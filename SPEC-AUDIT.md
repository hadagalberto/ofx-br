## Re-audit after the milestone-8 independent review

An independent reviewer found defects that the previously green suite did not catch. All are
fixed, each with a failing-first regression test, and summarized in
[`docs/REVIEW-FIXES.md`](./docs/REVIEW-FIXES.md):

- CLI dedupe/merge used the raw FITID as its key and discarded distinct rows; the key is now
  account + date + amount + memo.
- `0.125`-style amounts were read as thousands; leading-zero integer parts are now decimals and
  genuinely ambiguous `10.500`-style values warn.
- CDATA sections, non-nestable sibling balances (`LEDGERBAL`/`AVAILBAL`) and repeated leaves are
  parsed and reported instead of truncated or silently dropped.
- Fractional timezone offsets, counterparty cleanup, numeric business names and card-sign
  transparency were corrected; missing FITIDs and rewritten signs now warn.
- Property tests assert real invariants; the fixture corpus tautology was replaced; CSV output
  neutralizes formula injection; the CLI reads stdin via `-` and rejects invalid `--tz` with exit 2.

No requirements remain open.
